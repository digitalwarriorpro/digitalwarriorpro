Features

Everything your disc needs

From registration to retirement, DiskTrek tracks every moment in a disc's life.

QR Code Registration

Each disc gets a unique, waterproof-printable QR code that links to its full digital profile. One scan reveals everything.

  • Instant QR generation
  • Printable sticker format
  • Owner contact info

Course History

Log every course your disc has visited. See a full map of its journey across courses worldwide.

Score Tracking

Record round scores per disc. Analyze which disc performs best on which course type.

Player History

Know every hand that's thrown this disc. Tag players each round and build a community disc network.

Lost & Found

When someone finds your disc, they scan it and see your contact info instantly. No more lost discs.

UDisc Integration

Sync your rounds from UDisc automatically. Import courses, scores, and player data seamlessly.
